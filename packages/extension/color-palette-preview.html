Key Design Decisions

  • Two distinct oranges: Yellow-orange #ff9944 for keywords, Red-orange #ff6644 for output params
  • Two distinct blues: Cyan-blue #44aaee for parameters, Violet-blue #9988ff for booleans
  • Functions: Lime green - distinct from everything else
  • No muddy corals: Clean, saturated oranges instead
  • No pure primaries: All colors shifted off #ff0000, #00ff00, #0000ff, #00ffff
